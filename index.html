Five models that predict which phone customers will cancel. I scored them on how many cancellations they caught, because missing someone who leaves is the expensive mistake.

Case study 03

Photo recognition

A practice set of small photos in 10 categories, such as cars and birds. A small network got 65.4% right. Altered training photos raised that to 71.8%. A network already trained on other photos reached 87.1%.
