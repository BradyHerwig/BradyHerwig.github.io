Case study 01

NamUs Missing Persons Analysis

How do demographic and geographic characteristics of NamUs missing-person listings differ between juveniles and adults—and which factors best distinguish the two groups when modeled with logistic regression versus a decision tree?

Classification EDA Scikit-learn Public data

Slide 1 of 4

What it shows

Patterns in public listings

End-to-end analysis of public NamUs missing-person listings: clean the extract, explore juvenile vs adult profiles, then classify age_group from sex, state, and race/ethnicity only—never age, which would leak the label. Logistic regression led on overall accuracy; the tree recovered more juveniles. Results describe patterns in a listing database, not true risk of going missing.

What I learned

Metrics that matter

Accuracy alone can hide minority-class failure. Comparing an additive logistic model to a tree makes the cost of ignoring interactions concrete. Practiced leakage-aware features, stratified evaluation, and careful framing of sensitive public data.
